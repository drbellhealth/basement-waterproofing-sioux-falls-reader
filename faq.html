Basement Waterproofing in Sioux Falls, SD — FAQ

Honest answers to the questions homeowners most commonly ask before scheduling basement waterproofing or foundation repair work in the Sioux Falls area.

What's it going to cost?

$3,500 to $12,000 for most jobs. Median lands around $5,500 to $7,500 on a typical Sioux Falls basement. Crack repair alone is cheaper — $450 to $900 per crack. Get the written quote before any work starts.

When should I get the work done?

Anytime the ground isn't frozen. Inside work runs year-round. Outside excavation waits for the thaw — late April through October. Book early if you can; everyone calls in April when their basement leaks.

Why does it only leak in spring?

Snowmelt sits on top of still-frozen ground. The water can't drain down, so it pushes sideways against your wall. It finds the easiest crack and comes through. June's drier soil hides the problem — it'll be back next April.

Permit for an egress window?

Yes. City of Sioux Falls requires it. Your installer pulls it for you. The window has to meet code — 5.7 square feet opening, 24 inches tall minimum, 44-inch max sill height.

How long does a sump pump last?

7 to 10 years for the pump. 4 to 5 for the backup battery. Replace it before it dies, not after a flood. $700 to $1,400 for the replacement; way more if you wait.

Insurance?

Usually no for seepage and groundwater. Yes for sudden plumbing failures and storm damage. Sewer backups need a separate rider. Call your carrier.

Interior or exterior drain tile?

Interior, unless the wall itself is failing. Cheaper, faster, no torn-up landscaping. Same warranty. Exterior is for bowing walls and structural failures.

Warranty?

Lifetime transferable on waterproofing. 25 years on structural foundation work. Transferable means it goes to the next owner if you sell.

Basement is flooding right now. What do I do?

Cut power to the affected outlets if you can reach the breaker safely. Move valuables off the floor. Photograph everything. Call. Don't start mopping until someone identifies the source.

What if I just leave it alone?

It gets worse and more expensive every year. Mold starts in 48 hours. Wall concrete pits and spalls. A $5,000 fix becomes a $12,000 fix in five years. Don't wait.
